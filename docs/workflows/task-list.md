# Workflows the team does, by how often

The top 20 (1–20) must be completable with the keyboard alone. Budgets for each live in `specs/`.

## Constant (100+/day)
1. Search/open a patient
2. View today's schedule / switch provider or op view
3. Update appointment status (arrived → seated → ready → out)
4. Glance at patient summary (alerts, balance, insurance, next appt)
5. Identify an inbound caller (caller ID → chart)
6. Send/read patient texts
7. Write clinical/progress notes
8. Chart existing conditions and findings

## Very frequent (30–100/day)
9. Book an appointment
10. Move/reschedule an appointment
11. Set procedures complete (post production)
12. Take a patient payment
13. Confirm appointments
14. View/attach x-rays and intraoral photos
15. Review/update medical history
16. Schedule next hygiene visit at checkout
17. Explain a balance / show the ledger
18. Estimate patient portion
19. Handle cancellations and no-shows

## Frequent (10–30/day)
20. Verify insurance eligibility and benefits
21. Build a treatment plan
22. Present treatment / get acceptance
23. Sign consent forms
24. Create and send claims
25. Attach x-rays or narratives to claims
26. Fill openings from the ASAP/short-call list
27. Update demographics or contact info
28. Staff-to-staff message or task
29. Perio charting
30. Collect/import online intake forms
31. Enter or scan a new insurance card

## Daily (a few/day)
32. New patient setup
33. Post insurance payments (ERA/EOB)
34. Write prescriptions
35. Create/track lab cases
36. Morning huddle report
37. Work recall and unscheduled treatment lists
38. Pre-authorizations
39. Patient financing application
40. Adjustments and write-offs
41. Referral out letter
42. End-of-day close, deposit, reconciliation
43. Review request after visit
44. Clock in/out

## Weekly/monthly
45. Claim follow-up / insurance aging
46. Denied claim appeals
47. Patient statements
48. Refunds
49. Production/collection/KPI reports
50. Schedule template and provider hour edits
51. Merge duplicate patients
52. Inventory ordering
53. Fee schedule updates
54. Month-end close reports
