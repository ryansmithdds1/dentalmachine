# Data Retention and Disposal Policy

**Owner:** [Security lead] · **Reviewed:** yearly

- Patient records belong to the practice. They are kept while the practice is a customer and for as long as
  its state requires dental records to be kept (commonly 7–10 years after the last visit; longer for minors).
- Audit logs are kept at least 6 years (HIPAA documentation rule).
- Backups roll off after their retention period (14 days by default).
- When a practice leaves, it receives a complete export; its data is deleted from production within 30
  days and from backups when they roll off, and deletion is confirmed in writing.
- Call recordings and transcripts, when a practice turns recording on, follow the same rules; the AI scribe
  never stores the conversation.
- Paper and devices holding customer data are destroyed securely (certified shredding, disk wiping).
