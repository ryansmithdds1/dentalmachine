# How do I check that backups ran?

**Who:** office manager · **Where:** Settings → Backups · **How often:** About monthly

Shows when the last backups ran and whether they worked.

## Steps

1. Click Settings (bottom of the menu), then "Backups"

   ![Step 1](../../client/public/manual/img/A159-01.webp)

## If something goes wrong

- **A backup failed** — It is also listed in Needs attention ([A049](A049-work-the-needs-attention-list.md)). Contact support if it keeps failing.
